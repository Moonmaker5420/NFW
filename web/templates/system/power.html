System Power

Reboot or power off the firewall.

Reboot restarts the system — services go down for ~30–60 seconds, then come back automatically.
Power off shuts the system down completely. It will not come back unless you physically power it on (or your hypervisor restarts it).

Before you reboot: any in-flight captive portal sessions are preserved in the database, and active VPN peers will need to reconnect. Any configuration staged but not applied will be lost.
